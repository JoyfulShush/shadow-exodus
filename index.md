<summary><p style="font-size:20px;display:inline;font-weight:bold;cursor:pointer">SE#01: Attention Seeker</summary>

### Preconditions Unlocked In This Mission:
* Complete the mission by destroying the GDI base before the GDI reinforcements arrive.
	* Unlocks the precondition **GDI Base Destroyed Quickly?** in [SE#02](#se02).

### Preconditions Used In This Mission:
* None.

</details>

<details id="se02" style="margin-bottom: 20px">
<summary><p style="font-size:20px;display:inline;font-weight:bold;cursor:pointer">SE#02: The Humanitarian Crisis</summary>

### Preconditions Unlocked In This Mission:
* Intercept the GDI armored truck that spawns from the west entrance some time after the sixth convoy entered the map, before it exits through the winding way to the north.
	* Unlocks the precondition **Mammoth Blueprints Captured?** in [SE#03](#se03) and [SE#11](#se11).

### Preconditions Used In This Mission:
* **GDI Base Destroyed Quickly?** - Whether the GDI base was destroyed before GDI reinforcements arrived in [SE#01](#se01).
	* Effect: Grants the player 4 Elite Recon Bikes from the south some after after the third convoy is destroyed or reaches the village successfully.

</details>

* * *
   
<details id="se03" style="margin-bottom: 20px">
<summary><p style="font-size:20px;display:inline;font-weight:bold;cursor:pointer">SE#03: False Eagle</summary>

### Preconditions Unlocked In This Mission:
* Destroy all 4 civilian power plants to the east inside the city.
	* Unlocks the precondition **Civilian Power Station Destroyed?** in [SE#04](#se04).

### Preconditions Used In This Mission:
* **Mammoth Blueprints Captured?** - Whether the Mammoth Blueprint crate was picked up after intercepting the GDI convoy in [SE#02](#se02).
	* Effect: Grants the player 3 GDI Mammoth Tanks from the west, some moderate time after setting up a base.

</details>

* * *

<details id="se04" style="margin-bottom: 20px">
<summary><p style="font-size:20px;display:inline;font-weight:bold;cursor:pointer">SE#04: Shakedown</summary>

### Preconditions Unlocked In This Mission:
* Destroy the blue signal jammer in the swamp to the northeast.
	* Unlocks the precondition **Radar Jammer Destroyed?** in [SE#05](#se05).

### Preconditions Used In This Mission:
* **Civilian Power Station Destroyed?** - Whether all civilian power plants were destroyed in [SE#03](#se03).
	* Effect: Spawns periodic local militia reinforcements to the southwest island, starting some moderate time after the game starts. The reinforcements are allied to the player, and consists of 3 Rangers, 2 Allied Tanks, 2 Rifle infantry and 3 Rocket Soldiers. Those units are AI controlled and look for enemy targets to attack.

</details>

* * *

<details id="se05" style="margin-bottom: 20px">
<summary><p style="font-size:20px;display:inline;font-weight:bold;cursor:pointer">SE#05: Talons' Grasp</summary>

### Preconditions Unlocked In This Mission:
* Capture the damaged Soviet Forward Command Center to the north among the ruined Soviet base.
	* Unlocks the precondition **Old Command Center Captured?** in [SE#06](#se06).

### Preconditions Used In This Mission:
* **Radar Jammer Destroyed?** - Whether the signal jammer was destroyed in [SE#04](#se04).
	* Effect: Increases the time that the player has to prepare for GDI before they arrive.

</details>

* * *

<details id="se06" style="margin-bottom: 20px">
<summary><p style="font-size:20px;display:inline;font-weight:bold;cursor:pointer">SE#06: Reduction in Force</summary>

### Preconditions Unlocked In This Mission:
* Capture the Allied Tech Center located in the final Vanguard base to the northeast.
	* Unlocks the precondition **Enforcer Blueprints Captured?** in [SE#10](#se10) and [SE#11](#se11).

### Preconditions Used In This Mission:
* **Old Command Center Captured?** - Whether the Soviet Forward Command Center was captured in [SE#05](#se05).
	* Effect: Grants the player 3500 credits some time after capturing the Soviet War Factory.

</details>
